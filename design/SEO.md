# Pairspace SEO plan

## Positioning phrase
Participant Matching for Events. Never dating, never networking, never event management.

## Keyword clusters

### Head terms (title, h1, opening paragraph)
* participant matching for events
* event participant matching
* partner matching for events
* let participants find a partner
* participant partnering

### Body terms (section headings and copy)
* find a partner at an event
* dance partner matching for workshops
* leader and follower matching
* acro partner finder for festivals
* workshop partner matching tool
* event partner finder link
* match participants by category
* participant matching software for organizers

### Long tail (FAQ, use cases, alt text)
* how to help participants find a partner at a workshop
* how do dance events balance leaders and followers
* acro festival base and flyer matching
* partner matching without an app
* temporary event profile for participants
* notify participants when a matching partner joins
* share one event link for partner finding

### Negative terms to avoid on the page
dating, singles, hookup, social network, friendship app, event management suite, ticketing

## Metadata

Title (58 chars)
`Pairspace | Participant Matching for Events`

Description (154 chars)
`Create an event, define participant categories such as Base, Flyer, Leader or Follower, and share one link. Participants partner up themselves. No app needed.`

Open Graph title
`The easiest way to let participants partner up.`

## Structured data
* `SoftwareApplication` for the product with `applicationCategory: BusinessApplication` and a free offer.
* `FAQPage` for the FAQ block.
* `BreadcrumbList` on legal and content pages.
* `Organization` in the root layout.

## On page checklist
* One h1, the hero headline.
* Section h2 headings carry body terms verbatim.
* Every interface preview has descriptive alt text or aria labels with a keyword.
* Internal links from the landing page to how it works, use cases, privacy and the event creation flow.
* `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` generated by the app router.
* Canonical URL set from `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`.
* Event pages are `noindex` because participant data must never be crawled.
